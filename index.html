01 About

From a millisecond event to a lasting insight.

My research uses high-speed imaging, precision experiments and computational fluid dynamics to uncover the mechanisms hidden inside short-lived interfacial events.

I earned my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at KAUST, where my dissertation connected drop-impact crater collapse with singular jetting, acoustic emission and polymer-filament formation. My broader work spans petroleum systems, carbon capture and multiphase transport.

02 Research

Where interfaces move, collapse and speak.

My work combines controlled experiments with quantitative imaging to resolve nonlinear fluid events across scales.

Drop impact & singular jets

Fluid acoustics

Advanced flow diagnostics
